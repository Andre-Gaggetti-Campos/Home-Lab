## So, how do I 3d model?

To my knowledge, 3d modeling is a diverse field that deals with practively anything digital (or pre-digital version of a physical model).

For my purposes I will primarily focus on CAD software with the purpose of 3d printing.

Let's see what people recommend software-wise.

Fusion 360
- Pros:
    - Good for functional parts
    - Free with limitations
- Cons:
    - Paid
    - Steeper learning curve

Tinkercad
- Pros:
    - Beginner friendly
    - Quick simple models
    - Free
- Cons:
    - Limited precision
Blender
- Pros:
    - Very powerful
    - Strong sculpting abilities
    - Free
- Cons:
    - Not CAD-based
    - Difficult

Free Cad
- Pros:
    - Open source
    - Free
    - Precise
- Cons:
    - Less polished
    - Sharper learning curve

After some deeper research, watching some videos of each program in used and asking around in 3d printing communities, I will be continuing with Fusion 360. It's free for me, still generally applicable for my intents, and is not as difficult to progress in as Blender. Therefore, it strikes quite a nice balance.
